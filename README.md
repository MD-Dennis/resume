# Dennis Kipngetich
### AI Safety & Evaluation | LLM Quality | Prompt Engineering

I work at the intersection of AI evaluation, data quality, and model safety, helping teams identify failure modes, improve evaluation reliability, and build more dependable AI systems.

With 8+ years of experience in AI data annotation and quality assurance, my work spans LLM response evaluation, rubric development, reviewer calibration, dataset validation, multimodal assessment, and adversarial testing.

- **Evaluation Methodology:** Rubric design, reviewer calibration, benchmark quality, and structured feedback
- **Prompt Engineering:** Test-case design, multi-turn evaluation, and systematic analysis of model behavior

## Selected Experience
